# Tour

Rabbithole begins with reading, not organizing. A document fills the reader; a question asked at a passage becomes a neighboring document. The canvas is the same history viewed spatially.

The central object is a question asked at a place. The place may be a text range, an image region, a whole document, or the open canvas. The answer becomes another document, while the relationship remains visible and revisitable.

The product has two ways to supply answers. A local agent can listen through the MCP host, or the browser can ask a configured provider directly. They share the document rules and interface, so moving between them does not create a second product.

Frozen snapshots turn a Rabbithole into an inert, portable reading experience. They preserve the shape of the exploration without carrying live credentials, provider state, or mutation controls.

For the most direct introduction, open the interactive architecture tour beside this guide. It is a real frozen Rabbithole describing the system that produced it.
